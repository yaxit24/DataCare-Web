## Plain-data and recovery boundary

Both public functions accept `unknown`. Inputs must have complete exact shapes; extra keys, omitted required cells, accessors, custom prototypes, sparse arrays, cycles, symbols, nonfinite values and non-data values are refused. Ordinary and null-prototype records are detached before interpretation. Caller objects are not frozen or retained; results and their nested provenance are recursively frozen.

The profile bounds history to100 accepted commands, catalogs to1000 rows, recursion to24 levels, traversal to200,000 values and string-value text to8MiB. These are web profile limits, not inferred desktop limits. A rejected command returns a detached unchanged valid previous state; a malformed/inconsistent prior state is not echoed as valid. Unknown facts remain explicit diagnostics. Even accepted retention records its full semantic proof rather than silently dropping the command.

Review found a real output-closure defect: a valid8,388,608-character observation could append23 characters of proof and publish a state that the next call could not read.
