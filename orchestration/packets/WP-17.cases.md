## Goal

Turn the study of the menu Report → Cash Bank (`orchestration/work/WP-17/study.md`, 19 numbered gaps) into the
frozen case list. The next sessions write tests from your list and are measured against it, so each case needs
concrete steps and a concrete expected value derived from the desktop rule.

## Facts you need: the supervisor's decisions

The owner's standing rule is "the desktop first": the operator sees and does what the desktop does, and the web
adds only what an internet-hosted service needs to be safe.

1. **Scope.** Only the cash-and-bank mode, which is what the Lite edition reaches. The Scheme Ledger mode, its
   panel, the passbook print and the hidden JmUdr report are out of scope, as the study's Scope section cites.
2. **One account at a time, default `00001`.** As the desktop. The web's "every account whose code contains the
   text" behaviour is a gap.
3. **Date window, book filter, location and company year.** As the desktop, exactly as the study's query cites.
4. **Opening balance and running balance.** As the desktop: an opening row first, then a balance with its Cr or
   Dr side on each row, then the totals. Every expected figure is worked out in the case from the seeded
   vouchers: show the arithmetic.
5. **Print and Excel rights.** The web refuses Print and every export for a user without the Print right, with
   the desktop's message text where the desktop has one. If the desktop's Print button has no check, this is a
   deliberate difference: say so in the case.
6. **Drill-down.** A case for each voucher kind whose web screen exists in the Lite edition (sales, purchase,
   cash and bank vouchers, contra, sales order); a book whose voucher form the study could not confirm is
   `blocked` with that reason.
7. **Print layout.** The `AcLedger` template's regions and columns are the expected output. Read its XML under
   `extraction_output/rpt_xml/` only as far as you need to name the regions and columns; if the study says a part
   was not read, the case that needs it is `blocked` with that reason.
8. **The study's open questions** (the Ctrl+F11 entry, editability of the name box, print opening versus grid
   opening): a case that depends on one of them is `blocked` with that reason, and the question is repeated under
   "Not covered" for the owner.

## What to cover

- Every one of the 19 gaps in the study has at least one case that fails today because of it. Name the gap number
  in the case.
- Behaviour that is already right also gets a case, so a later change cannot break it unnoticed.
- For a report, T07 to T10 and T13 do not apply to the report itself: mark them `N/A` with that reason. T03
  (the opening, running balance and totals arithmetic), T16 (output), T11 (scope and rights: a second tenant, a
  second location, a user without the right), T04 (the account lookup and its help list) and T17 (keyboard)
  carry most of the cases. T18 is a journey: a cash receipt, a cash payment, a bank receipt and a contra are
  entered through their own screens, and the report for the cash account and for the bank account shows each of
  them with the right side, balance and totals.
- The module id is `cash-bank-report`. Case ids look like `cash-bank-report.balance.opening-row`.

## Out of scope

- Writing tests or code. Changing the study. The Scheme Ledger mode.
