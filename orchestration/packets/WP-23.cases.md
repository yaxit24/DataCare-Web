## Goal

Turn the study of Company → Balance TrFr (`orchestration/work/WP-23/study.md`, 21 numbered gaps) into the frozen case
list. This menu moves money, gold fine, silver fine and stock into the next year, and a wrong carry is silent, so the
centre of the list is database and browser cases that prove **closing(year N) = opening(year N+1)** per party and
`DATA_TYPE`, per item, per tag, per open order and per open approval, with every expected figure worked out from the
seeded vouchers using the desktop formulas.

## Facts you need: the supervisor's decisions on the study's questions

Standing rule: "the desktop first"; the web adds only what a hosted multi-user service needs to be safe and correct.

1. **Datasets in scope:** the five Lite datasets (Ac Balance, Item Stock, Tag Stock, Sales Order, Approval). Lite
   companies keep the default blank `MsMfgType`, so `lblJadtarTrfr` and `btnMfgTrfr` stay hidden; Scheme, Dhiran,
   Loan, Bill Os and the other buttons are `N/A` because their menus are not Lite (cite the study's table). The web
   page must not offer them: one case.
2. **Source rows, grouping, metal and skip rules, rounding (gaps 1 to 6):** exactly the desktop's: parties only
   (`AC_MAIN_GR < '3'`), up to the closing date, one opening per account and `DATA_TYPE`, gold and silver fine by
   `IT_TYPE`, the zero-skip rule, and rounding of the grouped sum to 0.00 / 0.000 half away from zero (VB `Format`)
   computed in decimals, never binary floats. One case per rule, with numbers chosen to expose a float or grouping
   error (for example two vouchers summing to x.xx5).
3. **Where the opening lands:** assert what the operator sees, not a table: the carried row on the web's Party
   Opening / Item Opening screens for year N+1 and the opening line of the Ledger Report for year N+1. Find which
   table the web Party Opening and Item Opening menus read and name it in the case.
4. **Re-run:** the desktop deletes and rewrites, so a second run **replaces** and never doubles. Delete scope is the
   target year only (the desktop deletes every year of the company and location: a weakness, deliberate difference).
5. **Atomicity:** keep the web's one transaction per button (better than the desktop). One case injects a failure
   after the first table and proves nothing of the carry remains.
6. **Tag Stock, Sales Order, Approval (gaps 10 to 12):** read the insert value lists the study left open
   (`BtmSchm.cs:2378`, `:3137`, `:4154`, `:4291`) yourself before writing their column-by-column expectations. The
   "Order Tag Booking" second pass (`:3226-3335`) and the question whether an approved-out tag appears in both the
   Tag Stock and the Approval carry: derive the expectation from the code; if the code does not settle it, the case
   is `blocked` with the exact question.
7. **Masters:** the web keeps one database across years, so the `AC_MAST` copy is `N/A`. `LAST_VISIT`/`VISIT_TRAN`
   updates and the closing-year side effects (`APR_DETL.ISU_REC`, `BAR_DETL.APR_ISU` recomputation) go under
   "Not covered", each with its citation, for the owner.
8. **Texts and keys:** the desktop's confirmation `Are You Sure To Data Transfer to Next Year ?`, completion
   `Process Are Complete....` and no-year message, verbatim; Esc closes. Rights: the catalog's create right.
9. **Print:** none on the desktop; `N/A`.
