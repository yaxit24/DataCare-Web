## Goal

Turn the study of Report → Stock Report (`orchestration/work/WP-16/study.md`, 17 numbered gaps) into the frozen case
list. Stock is built through real screens (Item Opening, Tag Opening, Purchase, Sale, Sales Return, Purchase Return,
Stock Adjust, Tag Cancel, Approval Isu), and after each one the report's opening, purchase, inward, outward, sale and
closing per dimension must equal the signed `MAIN_STOCK` movements worked out by hand. Quote every citation as
`File.cs:N {{text}}`, including ranges, from the start.

## Facts you need: the supervisor's decisions on the study's questions

Standing rule: "the desktop first"; the web adds only what a hosted multi-user service needs to be safe and correct.

1. **Route and caption.** The Stock Report gets its own route (`/reports/stock-report`) and the nav caption
   "Stock Report"; `/reports/tag-stock` stays the `RP_TAG` form (WP-15). One case for each.
2. **Data source.** Every figure is the sum of signed `MAIN_STOCK` movements, never a `BAR_DETL` snapshot (gap 6).
   Closing = Opening + every movement in the window, including the hidden transfer in/out columns; seed only the
   `TR_TYPE` values the study names and assert the identity per row and in the "Total :" row.
3. **Fine.** Use the desktop drill-down rule exactly: `KR_FINE` for `+` rows, `ITM_FINE` for `-` rows (gap 7), with
   numbers where the two differ. The shared web helper `onHandNet()` also feeds other screens: the report gets its
   own resolver; list the `onHandNet()` difference under "Not covered" as a separate defect for the supervisor.
4. **Questions the study left to you (1, 2, 3, 5):** read the cited lines yourself before writing those cases: the
   menu rights handler above `MDI_FORM.cs:8881`, `KeyPreview` in `InitializeComponent` (`RP_STOCK.cs:1535-2811`),
   the `UserPrint` default, and the `EMP ISU` arithmetic (`RP_STOCK.cs:4905-5000`). Approval to an employee
   (`EMP ISU`) exists in Lite: include one scenario. If the source does not settle a case, mark it `blocked`.
5. **Voucher zoom** targets only Lite voucher menus; others are `N/A` with the citation. The web opens a voucher
   only when the user has the target menu's Read right.
6. **Print and export.** Refused without the Print right. Templates: search `extraction_output/rpt_xml/` for the
   names the study lists; title, columns and totals are cases, layout fidelity goes under "Not covered".
7. **Settings.** Lite defaults; `MsCoStockMerge` company merge is out of Lite scope apart from one tenant-isolation
   case. The "Item Lot" dimension only if its setting is reachable in Lite; otherwise `N/A`.
8. The web's separate `stockValuation` report stays as it is; out of scope.
