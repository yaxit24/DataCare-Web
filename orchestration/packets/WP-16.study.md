## Goal

Write the study document for the menu **Report → Stock Report**. The desktop form is `RP_STOCK` (6,407 lines; the
catalog's main range is `RP_STOCK.cs:4277-5160`; template `ItmStkBalance.rpt`). Today the web maps this menu to
`/reports/tag-stock`, which is a different desktop form (`RP_TAG`, WP-15); the Stock Report needs its own route. Stock
truth is the signed movements in `MAIN_STOCK` (`ITM_SIGN`); fine = net x touch / 100. The next workers write the case
list and tests from your document.

## Facts you need

- Menu dispatch `MDI_FORM.cs:8883`; the form is also created at `MDI_FORM.cs:13957` (F3 from other screens): say
  how that entry differs.
- The web's stock primitives are in `web/apps/api/src/modules/stock/stock.ts` (`onHandNet()`); a closing-stock
  report exists in `web/apps/api/src/modules/reports/closingStock.ts`. State whether either already matches a
  desktop view.
- Quote every citation as `File.cs:N {{text}}`, including ranges and shared-form references, from the start.

## What to cover

1. Every summary dimension and view the operator can choose (item, group, product, location, karigar, ...), the
   query each builds, and its grouping.
2. Every filter and its condition; date range: opening, inward, outward, closing per row.
3. Weight columns (gross, net, less, fine, loss or ghat where shown) and their formulas with rounding; totals.
4. Drill-down to movements or tags; print and export templates and parameters; rights; keyboard.
5. Web today and Gaps, numbered.
