## Goal

The Lite menu Utilities → Data Refresh shows six buttons. The owner decisions D-REF-1 to D-REF-3 in
`docs/agent-prompts/lite/17-data-refresh.md` are final: nothing from the giant buttons is ported blindly; each
statement is classified first. **Old Field** is `btnFildUpdate2_Click`, `MS_UPDATE.cs:9412` to `:32332` (about 23,000
lines), split into three parts. This packet classifies **part 3 of 3: lines 24700 to 32332** only. If the part starts or
ends inside a statement, include that whole statement in the part where it starts. The Field Update classification
(`orchestration/work/WP-24/field-update.md` on this branch) shows the expected format and many repeated statement
shapes: reuse its class decisions for identical shapes and cite them.

1. `## Scope`: the button, its range, the Print-right gate, the completion message verbatim.
2. `## Classification`: one row per UPDATE, INSERT, DELETE or ALTER the handler runs, in source order, in a table:
   `# | line | quoted statement fragment | table.columns written | condition | class | reason`.

4. `## Counts`: rows per class.
5. `## Open questions`.
